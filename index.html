Safety first

Fit30 Reset is educational software for generally healthy adults. It is not medical diagnosis, treatment or personalised clinical care.

Seek clinical advice before starting if you are pregnant or postpartum, under 18, frail, have or have had an eating disorder, kidney disease, uncontrolled diabetes, significant heart disease, recent surgery, or serious back or pelvic-floor problems.

Stop exercising and seek help if you notice chest pain, faintness, severe breathlessness, neurological symptoms such as numbness, weakness or changes in vision or speech, or sharp or radiating pain. In an emergency call 999 or 112.
